# Before class

## There is nothing to install

**Session 1 needs a browser.** No terminal, no Node.js, no Git, no GitHub, no downloads, and no API key. If you were expecting a setup evening, you get it back. (One optional thing worth two minutes — see below.)

## Do this

- Open **[icerynk.com](https://www.icerynk.com)** (the link is also in the Brightspace announcement), choose **Continue with Google**, and sign in with your **@nyu.edu** or **@stern.nyu.edu** account. Confirm you can open **Sessions → Session 1**. Personal Gmail accounts are not accepted. If you cannot get in, email clg236@nyu.edu before class.
- Review the [course syllabus](../../syllabus/syllabus.md).
- Bring a **charged laptop and charger**, and have your phone with you. Some of the morning runs on a phone.

## Nice to have, not required

Create an account at **[openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/)**. We use the site in the last forty minutes of the morning, and having the account already made means you can follow along on your own screen instead of only watching the projector.

Three things worth knowing before you try:

- **Signing up is free, and it is required even for the free models.** If you send a prompt without an account, the site asks you to sign up. "Free" means no payment — it does not mean no account.
- **Adding about $5 is recommended, not required.** The free models are rate-limited and often cut off mid-sentence. A few dollars removes that, and you will not spend most of it — six cases on a cheap model costs a fraction of a cent. You can complete every graded thing in this course for $0 if you prefer; you just note the limitation.
- **If the cost is a barrier at all, email clg236@nyu.edu.** There is an alternative and it is not a penalty. Do not skip the assignment over five dollars.

If you do not get to it, that is fine — creating the account is the lunch task on Tuesday and it takes about two minutes.

## What is *not* required

You do not need a GitHub account, a terminal, an installed agent, or an API key for Session 1, Session 2, or Project 1. We teach the terminal, Git, GitHub, and coding agents in **Session 4**, and the repository submission route starts with **Project 2**, after it has been taught.

If a message, an older version of this page, or a classmate tells you to install something for Tuesday, it is out of date. Ask before you spend an evening on it.
