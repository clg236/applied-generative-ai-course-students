# Before class

## Do this during lunch

- **Make sure you can sign in at [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/).** A free account is the only setup for this afternoon. **No install, no API key.** About $5 of credit is recommended but optional; free models work.
- Open **openrouter.ai/chat** once while you are signed in and confirm the page loads. Free models still require an account: sending a prompt while signed out triggers a signup modal. Free means no payment, not no signup.
- If you cannot sign in, bring the exact error or the screen you are stuck on. Do not create a second account to work around it.
- Read the [Field Evidence Sprint](../../activities/session-1/field-to-agent.md). It contains the field task, safety boundary, return clock, evidence packet, and in-room handoff.
- Prepare a phone or camera for privacy-safe field evidence only. Raw or identifiable media stays on your own device.
- Wear comfortable shoes and bring water. The field mission runs from 3:30 to 4:15, including the walk back.

## Install nothing

Week 1 runs entirely in a browser. **There is no terminal, Node, Git, GitHub, fork, clone, or coding agent this afternoon.** That toolchain is taught in **Session 4 on Tuesday, August 4**, and instructions go out the weekend before. Please do not work ahead on it — you would be setting up for a lab that is a week away.

## What the afternoon needs from you

The field mission is pair work. You do not need a solution before class; arrive ready to observe one visible information or decision problem and separate what you see from what you infer.

Project 1 is presented by pairs in three minutes at the start of Friday, July 31, with the evidence uploaded to **Brightspace** as one PDF or one zip beforehand. It is not a repository submission.
