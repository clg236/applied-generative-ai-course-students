# In class

## What we will do

1. Look at what a language model is actually doing — next-token prediction from weights plus the context you assembled — and why nothing in that loop checks a claim.
2. Put **two models on one identical prompt** in the OpenRouter playground at **openrouter.ai/chat**: one confirmed open-weights model and one closed model. Read the difference on format, length, invented specifics, refusals, and price per million tokens. A `(free)` label does not by itself mean open weights.
3. Write a three-line **switch card** — SHIP, SWITCH IF, NEVER. Keep it; its decision logic expands into Project 1's six fixed cases and three controlled runs.
4. Trace a business use case from task to candidate output, workflow, decision, and outcome.
5. Work in pairs on the [Field Evidence Sprint](../../activities/session-1/field-to-agent.md). Stop collecting at **4:05 PM** and be seated in KMC 2-70 by **4:15 PM**, even if the evidence packet is incomplete.
6. Sort the packet into **Observed**, **Interpreted**, and **Unknown**, then remove anything identifying.
7. Write the smallest testable version before starting the formal Project 1 runs: task sentence, input owner, correct-answer rule, “can't tell” rule, output reviewer, and non-AI comparison. A field trial is an informal experiment, not the baseline or either eval.
8. Ask another pair to break the idea, revise one thing, and start the Project 1 hand-in checklist.
9. Write [Build Log 1](../../assignments/build-logs/brief.md) individually during the final twenty minutes.

The class deck is a separate course material. The activity brief contains the complete collection and safety instructions.

## Leave with

- Two models on one prompt, compared, and a written switch card you can defend.
- Four lines: what you saw, who it makes life harder for, what they do now, and what information or capability is missing.
- One bounded task sentence, correct-answer rule, “can't tell” case, human check, and non-AI comparison.
- One peer challenge and one recorded revision.
- Your own copy of the organized pair packet and a scheduled next work time.
- Build Log 1 written and submitted to Brightspace.

## What you do not need

No terminal, no installs, no repository, no fork, no branch, no commit, and no API key. Just a browser and your OpenRouter account — a few dollars of credit is recommended but optional. The source-control and coding-agent workflow starts in **Session 4 on Tuesday, August 4**.

Do not paste identifiable people, private information, credentials, or claims your evidence does not support into a hosted model. A hosted model is somebody else's computer, and there is no unsend.
